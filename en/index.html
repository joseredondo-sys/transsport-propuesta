Special luggage

No check-in queues. No excess baggage. No carrying the box.

TransSport specialises in shipping sports equipment: the big or delicate gear that is a hassle to take with you, such as bikes, golf clubs, skis, snowboards or kites.

Airlines call it “special luggage”. For us, it is the only thing we ship. As athletes ourselves, we know what your equipment is worth and we look after it as if it were our own.

What travels with you?

Cycling

Road, MTB, triathlon and e-bikes. Sent dismantled, partly dismantled or fully assembled.

Ski & snowboard

Alpine, cross-country, telemark and snowshoes, delivered to your hotel at the resort.

Golf

Golf bags and pitch & putt sets, with group discounts for friends and tournaments.

Surf & kitesurf

Boards, kites, wakeboards, kayaks and canoes, also to the Balearic and Canary Islands.

We also ship climbing gear, parachutes and wheelchairs. No barriers to sport.

We take care of everything. You just enjoy.

  1. 01

    Ask for a quote

    Tell us what you are sending, from where, to where and by when. You will hear back within 48 hours.

  2. 02

    We bring the packaging

    Accept by email or phone, pay by bank transfer and we send you a box made for your equipment.

  3. 03

    We collect at your door

    At home, at work or at your building's reception. You sign the handover and we take it from there.

  4. 04

    Delivered on time

    To your hotel or the race start on the agreed day: up to 48 hours within Spain, around 5 days abroad.
